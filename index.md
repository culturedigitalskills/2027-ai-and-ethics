This lesson is for arts and humanities researchers who want to use AI responsibly in their research. It is also for the librarians, data service staff and research support teams who help them. No technical background is needed.

The lesson is an output of the [Connecting Arts and Humanities Infrastructures for AI Capability](https://artsandhumanities.ai) project funded by UKRI under IS8.

By the end of the lesson learners will be able to think through the ethics of using AI in their research, including:
- what does responsible AI mean for my research?
- whose permission do I need?
- will AI repeat the biases in my sources?
- how do I work with sensitive material safely?
- what goes in my ethics application?

Hands-on exercises help them make informed, responsible choices about using AI with cultural and research data.

The lesson has five short episodes of about 25 minutes each (10 minutes teaching and 15 minutes exercises). Each episode stands alone, so they can be taught separately or in any order.
